# Commercial-video report repair brief

- Reader: content-business owner, especially e-commerce short videos and brand-marketing assets. Chinese final research report, cutoff 2026-09-07.
- Required subjects: Kling, Runway and Google Veo services; commercial positioning, actual access by surface/region/tier, cost definitions, workflow fit, commercial/data-use restrictions and reliability evidence.
- Preserve all prior follow-ups: announcements are not ordinary-customer access; subscription/credits/API charges are not interchangeable; compute only a transparently comparable example; explain which rights, region or delivery-stability conditions overturn each tentative choice.
- Original final target approximately 3,500–5,000 Chinese characters; later feedback asks for more depth and less compression. Expand decision-relevant reasoning, not filler or new fixed template requirements.
- Initial thesis: choose a production workflow and contract surface, not one abstract winning model; Kling is a candidate for image-led e-commerce variation, Runway for editing existing assets, Veo for audio-bearing scene generation, subject to tested acceptance and contractual fit.
- Unit plan, in drafting order: 01 recommendation and real access (~20%); 02 production workflows and limits (~25%); 03 comparable generation vs adopted-output costs (~20%); 04 commercial rights and training clauses by contract (~20%); 05 reliability and trial/stop decisions (~15%). Check each unit before drafting the next.
- Essential known omission to fix: Runway ordinary terms section 4.4 permits model-training use of inputs/outputs; enterprise third-party-model protection must not be generalized to all subscriptions. Likewise separate Kling consumer/paid/API contracts and Flow consumer vs Google Cloud terms.
- Source map: exact product feature/help/pricing pages; provider endpoint schemas for matched sample calculation; actual terms and regional eligibility pages; independent primary benchmark methodology for narrow preference evidence. No actual paid video generation is authorized or necessary for documentary research.
- Exclusions: declaring a benchmark winner without comparable historical snapshot; asserting uptime, acceptance rate, cost savings or a successful customer checkout that has not been measured; expanding into all competing video models.
- Acceptance: clear differentiated recommendations and reject conditions; visible section structure; data and rights boundaries affect actual choices; cost calculation has units, identical exposed parameters and remaining mismatches; no process trace language in report body.
