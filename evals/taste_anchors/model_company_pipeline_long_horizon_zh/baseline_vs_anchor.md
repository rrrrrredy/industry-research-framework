# Baseline versus taste anchor

The weak baseline for this case is not merely a short report. It can contain many files, sources, and headings while still failing the assignment.

## Weak baseline

- Opens with broad advice about building model capability instead of explaining how company pipelines are organized.
- Repeats industry commonplaces for eight companies without company-specific ownership, interfaces, or evidence boundaries.
- Lets the data chapter crowd out pretraining, post-training, Infra, evaluation, Serving, and product feedback.
- Lists publications and media channels in the body instead of synthesizing mechanisms.
- Loses follow-up corrections about leadership time boundaries, Harness, tables, concise insights, and professional wording.
- Appends a glossary or source list after the reader explicitly rejected both.
- Treats a local review pass or a partial draft as permission to tell the reader the final report is complete.
- Hides unresolved items in backstage state, so the reader cannot distinguish a draft from a finished delivery.
- Accumulates large progress logs, control scripts, manifests, or rollback machinery that do not improve the report.

## High-quality anchor

- Starts with specific insights, including company-level differences.
- Shows the complete pipeline through ownership, inputs, outputs, decision rights, and feedback loops.
- Gives data and annotation extra depth while preserving all other training and delivery stages.
- Separates company evidence from cross-company synthesis and leaves unknown reporting lines unknown.
- Explains how Harness and Coding Agent change data units, environments, evaluation, Serving, and the release bundle.
- Uses compact tables where repeated mappings are easier to compare visually.
- Keeps evidence traceable backstage without exposing a rejected glossary or reference appendix.
- Preserves all thirteen requirement turns in a small current requirement ledger.
- Makes the user-visible status agree with progress, global review, accepted limitations, and current artifact hashes.

## Review use

Compare the primary object first: can the reader reconstruct who owns each pipeline stage, how work crosses boundaries, and how failures return to the correct layer? Then inspect company specificity and late corrections. Delivery integrity is a hard gate; a polished report with a false completion claim still fails.
